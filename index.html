What is Agentic AI?

Agentic AI is a new generation of artificial intelligence that can independently work toward goals, use tools, solve problems, and adapt to changing situations.

🧠

Think

Understands goals and analyzes information to make intelligent decisions.

📋

Plan

Breaks complex tasks into smaller steps and creates action plans.

⚡

Act

Uses tools and systems to execute tasks and achieve objectives.

How Does It Work?

A simple Agentic AI workflow.

01. Receive a Goal

The user provides an objective or task.

02. Analyze & Plan

The AI understands the task and decides what steps are needed.

03. Execute Actions

The agent uses available tools to perform the planned actions.

04. Evaluate Results

It checks the results and adjusts its actions if necessary.
